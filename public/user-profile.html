Greek Salad

Easy 💰 Low
⏱️ 15 min ★★★★☆ 4.2 (12 ratings)
▼
Ingredients:
2 tomatoes · 1 cucumber · 100g feta · black olives · red onion · olive oil · oregano
Steps:
  1. Chop tomatoes, cucumber and onion into chunks.
  2. Add olives and crumbled feta.
  3. Drizzle with olive oil and sprinkle oregano. Serve immediately.

Shrimp Pasta

Medium 💰💰 Medium
⏱️ 30 min ★★★★★ 4.8 (5 ratings)
▼
Ingredients:
300g pasta · 200g shrimp · 3 cloves garlic · olive oil · white wine · parsley · salt & pepper
Steps:
  1. Cook pasta according to package instructions.
  2. Sauté garlic in olive oil, add shrimp and cook 2 min per side.
  3. Deglaze with white wine. Toss in pasta, season and garnish with parsley.

Grandma's Beef Stew

Medium 💰💰 Medium 🔒 Private
⏱️ 90 min
▼
Ingredients:
500g beef chuck · 3 potatoes · 2 carrots · 1 onion · beef broth · thyme · bay leaf
Steps:
  1. Brown beef in batches over high heat.
  2. Add vegetables and broth. Simmer for 75 min.
  3. Season and serve hot with crusty bread.
