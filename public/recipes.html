Spaghetti Carbonara

⏱️ 30 min 👤 2 servings
SM
@sofia.m
★★★★★
(24)
▼
🛒 Ingredients
200g spaghetti · 100g pancetta · 2 large eggs · 50g Pecorino Romano · 50g Parmesan · 2 cloves garlic · Black pepper · Salt
📋 Steps
  1. Cook spaghetti in salted boiling water until al dente.
  2. Fry pancetta with garlic until crispy, then remove garlic.
  3. Whisk eggs with grated cheeses and plenty of black pepper.
  4. Off the heat, toss hot pasta with pancetta, then quickly stir in egg mixture.
  5. Add pasta water to loosen the sauce. Serve immediately.

Avocado Toast with Poached Egg

⏱️ 15 min 👤 1 serving
JB
@jake.b
★★★★★
(41)
▼
🛒 Ingredients
2 slices sourdough bread · 1 ripe avocado · 2 eggs · Lemon juice · Chilli flakes · Salt & pepper · Fresh parsley
📋 Steps
  1. Toast the sourdough until golden and crisp.
  2. Mash avocado with lemon juice, salt, and pepper.
  3. Poach eggs in simmering water with a splash of vinegar for 3 minutes.
  4. Spread avocado on toast, top with poached egg.
  5. Garnish with chilli flakes and fresh parsley.

Chicken Tikka Masala

⏱️ 45 min 👤 4 servings
RA
@rashid.a
★★★★★
(53)
▼
🛒 Ingredients
500g chicken breast · 1 cup yogurt · 2 tbsp tikka masala paste · 1 onion · 2 cloves garlic · 1 tsp ginger · 400g canned tomatoes · 1/2 cup cream · Salt & pepper
📋 Steps
  1. Marinate chicken in yogurt and tikka masala paste for at least 30 mins.
  2. Sauté onion, garlic, and ginger until soft.
  3. Add marinated chicken and cook until sealed.
  4. Add tomatoes and simmer 15–20 mins.
  5. Stir in cream and serve with rice or naan.

Fluffy Pancakes

⏱️ 20 min 👤 2 servings
SR
@sophie.r
★★★★★
(36)
▼
🛒 Ingredients
150g all-purpose flour · 1 tbsp sugar · 1 tsp baking powder · 1/2 tsp baking soda · 1/4 tsp salt · 200ml milk · 1 egg · 30g melted butter
📋 Steps
  1. Mix dry ingredients in a bowl.
  2. Whisk milk, egg, and melted butter separately.
  3. Combine wet and dry ingredients until smooth.
  4. Cook pancakes on a lightly greased pan until bubbles form, flip and cook another 1–2 min.
  5. Serve warm with syrup or fresh fruits.

Chocolate Fudge Brownies

⏱️ 35 min 👤 8 servings
CM
@carl.m
★★★★★
(62)
▼
🛒 Ingredients
200g dark chocolate · 150g butter · 200g sugar · 3 eggs · 100g flour · 30g cocoa powder · 1 tsp vanilla extract · Pinch of salt
📋 Steps
  1. Melt chocolate and butter together until smooth.
  2. Whisk in sugar, then eggs one at a time.
  3. Add flour, cocoa powder, salt, and vanilla. Mix gently.
  4. Pour into a lined baking tray and bake at 180°C for 20–25 mins.
  5. Cool completely before cutting into squares.
