Spaghetti Carbonara

⏱️ 30 min 👤 2 servings
SM
@sofia.m
★★★★★
(24)
▼
🛒 Ingredients
200g spaghetti · 100g pancetta · 2 large eggs · 50g Pecorino Romano · 50g Parmesan · 2 cloves garlic · Black pepper · Salt
📋 Steps
  1. Cook spaghetti in salted boiling water until al dente.
  2. Fry pancetta with garlic until crispy, then remove garlic.
  3. Whisk eggs with grated cheeses and plenty of black pepper.
  4. Off the heat, toss hot pasta with pancetta, then quickly stir in egg mixture.
  5. Add pasta water to loosen the sauce. Serve immediately.

Avocado Toast with Poached Egg

⏱️ 15 min 👤 1 serving
JB
@jake.b
★★★★★
(41)
▼
🛒 Ingredients
2 slices sourdough bread · 1 ripe avocado · 2 eggs · Lemon juice · Chilli flakes · Salt & pepper · Fresh parsley
📋 Steps
  1. Toast the sourdough until golden and crisp.
  2. Mash avocado with lemon juice, salt, and pepper.
  3. Poach eggs in simmering water with a splash of vinegar for 3 minutes.
  4. Spread avocado on toast, top with poached egg.
  5. Garnish with chilli flakes and fresh parsley.

Chicken Tikka Masala

⏱️ 60 min 👤 4 servings
RP
@raj.p
★★★★★
(37)
▼
🛒 Ingredients
700g chicken breast · 400ml coconut cream · 2 onions · 4 cloves garlic · 1 tbsp ginger · Tikka paste · Cumin · Coriander · Garam masala · Tomato purée · Fresh coriander
📋 Steps
  1. Marinate chicken in tikka paste and yoghurt for at least 30 min.
  2. Grill or pan-fry chicken until charred; set aside.
  3. Sauté onions, garlic, and ginger until golden. Add spices and tomato purée.
  4. Pour in coconut cream, simmer for 15 minutes.
  5. Add chicken to the sauce, simmer 10 more minutes. Garnish and serve with rice.

2-Ingredient Banana Pancakes

⏱️ 10 min 👤 1 serving
EL
@emma.l
★★★★★
(58)
▼
🛒 Ingredients
1 ripe banana · 2 eggs · Pinch of cinnamon (optional) · Butter for the pan
📋 Steps
  1. Mash the banana thoroughly in a bowl.
  2. Add eggs and cinnamon; mix until combined.
  3. Heat butter in a non-stick pan over medium-low heat.
  4. Pour small circles of batter and cook 2 min per side until golden.
  5. Serve with maple syrup or fresh berries.

Classic Greek Salad

⏱️ 10 min 👤 2 servings
DC
@dina.c
★★★★★
(19)
▼
🛒 Ingredients
3 tomatoes · 1 cucumber · 1 red onion · 100g Kalamata olives · 150g feta cheese · Olive oil · Dried oregano · Salt
📋 Steps
  1. Chop tomatoes, cucumber, and red onion into large chunks.
  2. Combine in a bowl with olives.
  3. Drizzle generously with olive oil and season with salt.
  4. Top with a whole slab of feta and sprinkle oregano on top.
  5. Serve immediately — do not toss.

Crispy Beef Street Tacos

⏱️ 25 min 👤 3 servings
MV
@miguel.v
★★★★★
(62)
▼
🛒 Ingredients
500g beef mince · 6 small corn tortillas · 1 white onion · Fresh coriander · Lime · Cumin · Smoked paprika · Garlic powder · Salsa verde · Sour cream
📋 Steps
  1. Season beef with cumin, smoked paprika, garlic powder, salt.
  2. Cook beef in a hot pan, breaking it up until crispy and caramelised.
  3. Warm tortillas directly on the gas flame for 30 seconds each side.
  4. Load tortillas with beef, diced onion, and coriander.
  5. Finish with salsa verde, sour cream, and a squeeze of lime.
