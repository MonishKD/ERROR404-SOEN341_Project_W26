Overnight Oats

⏱️ 10 min 🌾 Gluten-Free · 🥛 Dairy-Free
▼
Ingredients: Rolled oats, almond milk, chia seeds, honey, berries
Steps:
  1. Add oats and chia seeds to a jar
  2. Pour in almond milk and stir well
  3. Drizzle honey on top
  4. Cover and refrigerate overnight
  5. Top with fresh berries before serving

Greek Salad

⏱️ 15 min 🥦 Vegetarian
▼
Ingredients: Cucumber, tomato, red onion, olives, feta, olive oil, oregano, salt
Steps:
  1. Cut cucumber, tomato, and red onion into chunks
  2. Add olives and crumbled feta
  3. Drizzle with olive oil
  4. Sprinkle oregano and salt
  5. Toss gently and serve

Classic Lasagna

⏱️ 75 min 🥛 Contains: Gluten, Dairy
▼
Ingredients: Lasagna sheets, ground beef, tomato sauce, béchamel, mozzarella, parmesan, garlic, onion
Steps:
  1. Brown beef with garlic and onion
  2. Add tomato sauce and simmer 15 min
  3. Prepare béchamel sauce separately
  4. Layer: pasta, meat sauce, béchamel, cheese
  5. Repeat layers and top with mozzarella
  6. Bake at 375°F for 45 minutes

Banana Ice Cream

⏱️ 5 min 🌱 Vegan · 🌾 Gluten-Free
▼
Ingredients: 2 frozen bananas (optional: peanut butter, cocoa powder, vanilla)
Steps:
  1. Peel and freeze bananas overnight
  2. Break into chunks and blend until smooth
  3. Add any optional mix-ins and blend again
  4. Serve immediately or freeze 30 min for firmer texture

Fluffy Pancakes

⏱️ 20 min 🥛 Contains: Gluten, Dairy, Eggs
▼
Ingredients: 1 cup flour, 1 tbsp baking powder, 1 cup milk, 1 egg, 2 tbsp butter, 1 tbsp sugar, pinch of salt, vanilla
Steps:
  1. Mix dry ingredients: flour, baking powder, sugar, salt
  2. Whisk wet ingredients: milk, egg, melted butter, vanilla
  3. Combine wet and dry, do not overmix
  4. Heat buttered pan over medium heat
  5. Pour ¼ cup batter per pancake, flip when bubbles form

Lemon Herb Chicken

⏱️ 45 min 🌾 Gluten-Free · 🥛 Dairy-Free
▼
Ingredients: Chicken thighs, lemon juice, garlic, rosemary, thyme, olive oil, salt, pepper
Steps:
  1. Mix lemon juice, garlic, herbs, olive oil, salt and pepper
  2. Marinate chicken for at least 30 minutes
  3. Preheat oven to 400°F
  4. Bake 35-40 minutes until golden and cooked through
  5. Rest 5 minutes before serving
